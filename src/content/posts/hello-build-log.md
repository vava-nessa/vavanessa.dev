This site is the workshop side of Vanessa Depraute's online presence: less formal than a CV, more durable than a social timeline, and focused on shipping useful developer tools in public.

The first wave of writing will cover open-source projects, product notes, small technical decisions, and the occasional opinionated field report.

The site is English-first because most distribution will happen through GitHub, Twitter/X, Reddit, Hacker News, and dev.to. French and Chinese translations can come later for specific articles when they are worth maintaining.
